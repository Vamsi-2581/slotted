## Against §18

1. **Purpose.** A time column, a date-time column, a date range as one control,
   and a long set as chips, each inside the same panel. Anti-use: nesting,
   presets over several columns and a quick filter, which stay `filter-bar`'s.
2. **Anatomy.** No part added. `filter-operand` carries `data-range` and
   `data-operand` beside `data-kind`; `filter.kinds` names seven kinds.
3. **Controlled and uncontrolled.** Unchanged: `value` / `defaultValue` /
   `onValueChange(value, request)` on the two new components; `model()` and
   `valueChangeRequest` in Angular.
4. **State matrix.** Unchanged. The operand attributes are shapes the panel
   took, not states a consumer moves, and the contract says so.
5. **Keyboard and ARIA.** Inherited from the four operand families, with the
   defect in the three pickers' `aria-label` forwarding recorded above.
6. **Recipes.** `size="sm"` on every operand, as the shipped kinds.
7. **Responsive, RTL, locale.** Captures at 240 and in RTL for all four scenes;
   `locale`, `hourCycle` and `timeZone` reach the pickers.
8. **Loading, empty, error, extreme.** An incomplete date-time is an inactive
   condition (`filter-datetime` scene, second picker). Twenty chips wrap inside
   the panel. A typed value outside `options` is refused and announced.
9. **SSR.** `useId` for the `datalist`; the Angular list id is generated once
   per instance.
10. **Examples.** Four scenes per page, in the dispatch-desk domain the page
    already uses.
11. **Shared behavioural tests.** React 38, Angular 25, the same four
    behaviours in each: the operand drawn, the ISO text written from a stepped
    segment, the range picker for `between` and the picker for `is`, the chips
    with a refused value.
12. **Break and migration.** None: `options` becomes optional, `value` widens
    by two union members, and no shipped prop changes meaning.
