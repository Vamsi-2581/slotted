# Detail panel — capability inventory

One record on screen: what identifies it, what state it is in, what can be
done to it, and what happened to it. The view a list row opens — in a drawer,
in a page of its own, or in the second pane of a split. The inventory is taken
from the field before any code, as
`.claude/skills/component/references/capability.md` requires; the delta
against what `page-header`, `tabs`, `section`, `drawer`, `timeline` and
`description-list` already ship is named in §5.

Three names hide one thing. sap.uxap's object page, Carbon's side panel and
Primer's page layout are one composition: a header that stays, a body of
sections, a column of metadata beside it, a footer of actions. What differs
is the host — a page, a slide-over, a split — and every surveyed product uses
all three for the same record.

## 3. Differentiators

| Capability                                                                          | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One record, three hosts, one anatomy                                                | The same `article` is the body of a `drawer`, of a `page` and of a `splitter` pane. Carbon's side panel and sap.uxap's page are two components with two APIs for one record; here the host supplies the surface and the panel never asks which one it is in. `scroll="ancestor" \| "self"` is the one thing a host changes.                                                                                                     |
| The chrome stacks under one property                                                | `page-header` publishes `--slotted-page-sticky-top` on the scroller; a stacked `section` sticks its header at that height; a `table` inside a section stops its column header under the section's. Primer needs `offsetHeader` stated by hand; here the number is measured once and read everywhere.                                                                                                                             |
| A request with a reason, and the layout does not change the model                   | `section` is one value in both layouts. Switching `layout` from `stacked` to `tabs` on a narrow host changes the picture and nothing the application wrote — the same value, the same handler, the same URL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| The record that is not there keeps its chrome                                       | `status="not-found"` and `status="error"` replace the body with `message` and keep the header, the back control and the footer, so a drawer opened on a deleted record still closes and a page still goes back. No surveyed implementation has a state for it; each product draws its own full-page error.                                                                                                                 |
| Focus follows the record                                                            | When `recordKey` changes while focus is inside the root, the heading takes focus and the scroller returns to its start. Linear's `J`/`K` moves the picture and leaves a screen reader on a control whose record changed under it.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Three schemes, three densities, right-to-left                                       | The aside, the footer rule and the section rhythm read the space scale; the header's controls the control scale; every inset is logical. Under `high-contrast` the aside and the footer are separated by the theme's black rule; under `forced-colors` the theme's remapping is enough and the family writes no rule of its own.                                                                                            |

## 4. Gaps

| Situation                                                       | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The record changed on the server while the reader has it open   | `banner`, a region between the header and the body, the width of both columns, where an `alert` says so with a reload action. Salesforce shows it as a toast that disappears; GitHub as a bar the reader has to scroll up to find. Here it sits under the sticky chrome, in the flow, so it is read before the stale content. |
| A record in the middle of loading is navigated away from        | `loading` keeps the strip and the section frames; the heading skeleton is `page-header`'s. The panel's `recordKey` changing during `loading` moves focus to the heading as it would otherwise.                                                                                                                                |
| A title of forty words, a value with no spaces                  | The heading is `page-header`'s and clamps at three lines with the full text in `title`. The aside's content takes `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, so an unbroken token wraps rather than widening the column.                                                                                                                       |
| Forty sections                                                  | Under `tabs` the strip scrolls with paging controls, which is `tabs`'s own overflow. Under `stacked` forty headings is forty regions and the reader jumps by landmark or heading; an anchor bar over them is `table-of-contents`'s, deferred.                                                                                         |
| A section with nothing in it                                    | The section renders its heading and the consumer's empty content — a `result-state` or one sentence. The family does not hide an empty section, because a missing "Attachments" heading reads as a record that cannot have attachments.                                                                                       |
| Two panels on one page                                          | Every id is generated per instance; a section's `id` is the `name` the consumer gave it only when they set `id` themselves, because two panels with a section named `activity` would otherwise collide on `#activity`.                                                                                                        |

## 5. Delta against what is already shipped

| Already shipped                                         | This family                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `page-header` — the whole title area and the tabs slot  | Composed as the first child; `level` and `size` defaults provided for it; the strip rendered from the sections into its `tabs` slot.                                                                     |
| `tabs` — the strip                                      | Composed for the list alone. The tab panels are the family's own `section` parts, wired to the tabs by the ids the strip's `identify` returns, because in Angular the sections are projected outside the strip's injector and a `[slTabPanel]` there cannot find its `[slTabs]`. |
| `section` — a region with a heading                     | Composed inside every `section` part under `stacked`, at `level + 1`, with `count` as its eyebrow and `actions` in its actions column.                                                                    |
| `skeleton` — the placeholder                            | Drawn inside each section under `loading`, as `page-header` draws it inside its heading.                                                                                                                 |
| `drawer`, `page`, `splitter`                            | The hosts. Nothing of theirs is rewritten: `open`, focus return, `Escape`, extent and scrolling are theirs.                                                                                               |
| `timeline`, `description-list`, `result-state`, `alert` | The content of a section, the aside, the message and the banner. Each is placed by the family and owned by the consumer.                                                                                 |
